I designed and fabricated a visuotactile sensor based on GelSight, introduced by Johnson and Adelson in 2009. Mounted on a robot gripper, it gives force information during grasping (normal and shear forces) and reconstructs the shape and texture of the contact surface with photometric stereo. That makes more delicate tasks possible, such as manipulating tape.

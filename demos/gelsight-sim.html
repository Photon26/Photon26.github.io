Physical effects

Every step from contact to pixel is modelled: an elastic gel layer, a semi-specular coating that casts shadows, LEDs with falloff and colour crosstalk, light scattered inside the gel, and a camera with parallax, blur and noise. Switch the effects off to see what each one adds.

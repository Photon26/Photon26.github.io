I design **compact vision-based tactile sensors**: cameras that image how a soft elastomer deforms, so a robot can measure the 3D shape of whatever it touches. My work covers the whole loop, from imaging models and optical design to prototyping, calibration, quantitative evaluation and integration on robot grippers.

I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from The University of Osaka in March 2026, at [Harada Lab](https://www.roboticmanipulation.org/) in the Graduate School of Engineering Science, supervised by Kensuke Harada and [Weiwei Wan](https://wanweiwei07.github.io/). Before coming to Japan in 2018, I studied physics at Zhejiang University (Chu Kochen Honors College).

## Three sensor generations

Each generation of my doctoral work tackled a different bottleneck: the illumination, the lens, and the narrow field of view of a thin sensor.
